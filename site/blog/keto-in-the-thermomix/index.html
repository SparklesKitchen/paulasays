Keto in the Thermomix: where to start.

Paula Wöbcke · 4 min read

Not everyone wants a plate of beef, butter and eggs, and that's fine. I eat meat-first, but a lot of you ask me about keto: lower carbs, plenty of good fat, and food that still feels like dinner. The Thermomix is brilliant at it, because so much of keto cooking is blending, emulsifying and gentle cooking.

Start with the staples

Cauliflower rice. Pulse a cauliflower in seconds, steam it, add butter. It takes minutes and it replaces the rice under a curry or a chilli.

Creamy sauces. A cheese sauce, a creamy mushroom sauce or a lemon hollandaise all come together in the bowl, with no lumps and no standing at the hob.

Homemade mayonnaise and dressings. Shop-bought versions often hide sugar. Yours takes a minute, and you decide what goes in.

Nut and seed butters. Smooth or crunchy, and no label to read.

"Keto doesn't have to mean boring. It means sauces, and the Thermomix loves a sauce." — Paula says

Where I look for recipes

Cookidoo has proper keto and low-carb collections, so I'm not guessing. The Keto collection says every recipe has under 15 g of net carbs per portion, and it includes things like cauliflower rice, creamy mushrooms, steak au poivre, cauliflower crust pizza, a low carb lasagne and a mushroom bolognese with courgette noodles. There's also a Keto Friendly collection with a lemon hollandaise, cloud bread and cream cheese pancakes. You need a Cookidoo subscription to cook from them, and I'd always check the numbers against what suits you.

My own approach is simple. Pick two sauces and one side, make them on Sunday, and build the week around meat, fish and eggs. If you'd like me to show you in your own kitchen, book a demo and bring your list.
