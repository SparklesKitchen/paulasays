The Keto Table · Nº 002 · The sauces

Three keto sauces in ten minutes.

Paula Wöbcke · 4 min read

If you only learn one thing about keto in the Thermomix, learn the sauces. A good sauce turns plain meat, fish or vegetables into dinner, and a lot of the ready-made ones come with sugar and starch you didn't ask for. The Thermomix makes the real thing in minutes.

1. Hollandaise, without the fear

Hollandaise has a reputation for splitting. In the Thermomix the temperature and the stirring are handled for you. Cookidoo has a Keto Lemon Hollandaise that takes about 10 minutes. Spoon it over eggs, asparagus or a piece of salmon.

2. Mayonnaise and its relatives

Mayonnaise is just egg, oil and a little acid, emulsified. It takes a minute in the bowl, and you choose the oil. Add garlic and you have aioli. Add mustard and herbs and you have a dressing for the week.

3. A peppery pan sauce

Cookidoo's Steak au Poivre is in their Keto collection and takes about 20 minutes. It is the kind of dinner that feels like a treat, and the sauce is the whole point. Serve it with Cauliflower Rice, also on Cookidoo, about 10 minutes.

"A sauce is how plain food gets its Saturday night." — Paula says

How I'd use them

Make one sauce when you need it, and use it on whatever the week throws at you: a steak, a grilled chicken thigh, steamed greens or a fried egg. Check each recipe's own storage advice on Cookidoo, because egg sauces are not all kept the same way.

Cookidoo says every recipe in its Keto collection has under 15 g of net carbs per portion. You need a Cookidoo subscription to cook from it, and I'd always check what suits you before you start. If you'd like to see a hollandaise made in front of you, book a demo.
