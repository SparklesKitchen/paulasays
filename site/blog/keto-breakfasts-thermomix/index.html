Keto breakfasts that aren't just eggs.

Paula Wöbcke · 4 min read

The hardest meal to do low-carb is breakfast. Toast, cereal, porridge and pancakes are all carbs. Eggs are brilliant, but not every morning. So here are three things on Cookidoo I'd look at first.

Keto Cloud Bread

A light, soft bread for your breakfast egg or a bit of butter. Cookidoo lists it at about 40 minutes, and it's in their Keto Friendly collection.

Keto Cream Cheese Pancakes

Another Keto Friendly recipe, around 40 minutes. A stack of these with butter and a few berries is the closest thing I've found to a lazy Sunday pancake morning.

Cheeseburger Quiche

This one lives in Cookidoo's Keto collection and takes about 50 minutes. It's more of a brunch or lunchbox dish. Make it once, slice it, and a few breakfasts are done.

"Make it once, eat it three times. That's the whole system." — Paula says

A note on the numbers

Cookidoo says every recipe in its Keto collection has under 15 g of net carbs per portion. The Keto Friendly collection doesn't promise that number, so check each recipe's nutrition information against what suits you. You need a Cookidoo subscription to cook from either.
